# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

Analysis of 3,900 retail transactions using **Python, PostgreSQL and Power BI** to understand customer spending and guide marketing decisions.

![Customer Behavior Dashboard](images/dashboard.png)

## Dashboard Highlights
- **3.9K customers**, **$59.76** average purchase, **3.75** average review rating
- Interactive filters for subscription status, gender, category and shipping type
- Views of revenue and sales by product category and by age group

## What I Did
- **Python:** cleaned the data, filled missing ratings, created age groups and purchase-frequency features
- **SQL:** answered 10 business questions on revenue, discounts, subscriptions, customer segments and top products
- **Power BI:** built the interactive dashboard above

## Key Findings
- Total revenue was **$233K**
- **Subscribers make up only 27%** of customers and spend no more than non-subscribers, so the subscription needs better benefits
- **Clothing** brings in the most revenue (45%), and **Young Adults** are the top-spending age group
- **80% of customers are loyal** (10+ past purchases), but only 83 are new, so acquisition is the gap
- **43% of orders use a discount**
